# Food Delivery Data Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project explores food delivery data to understand customer behaviour, restaurant performance, pricing patterns, and delivery operations.

Using Microsoft Excel, I transformed raw transaction data into an interactive dashboard to identify patterns affecting order demand, customer charges, restaurant ratings, and delivery performance.

The analysis focused on understanding how different factors contribute to the overall food delivery experience.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze order patterns across different weather conditions.
- Examine the relationship between traffic conditions and late deliveries.
- Compare restaurant-level performance, including average tax charges and customer ratings.
- Investigate service fees across customer segments.
- Compare restaurant preparation time with actual delivery time.
- Identify operational areas that may contribute to delivery delays.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- PivotCharts
- Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Interactive Dashboard Visualization
- Data Analysis and Interpretation

## Key Findings

### 1. Weather Conditions and Order Demand
Clear weather recorded the highest number of orders, indicating a pattern between weather conditions and customer ordering activity.

### 2. Traffic Conditions and Late Deliveries
Medium traffic recorded the highest number of late deliveries, highlighting the importance of monitoring traffic conditions when evaluating delivery performance.

### 3. Restaurant Charges and Ratings
The restaurant with the highest average tax charged also recorded a notable restaurant rating, revealing an interesting relationship worth further investigation.

### 4. Customer Segmentation and Service Fees
Premium customers were charged lower service fees than other customer segments. This raised questions about how customer segmentation and loyalty arrangements may influence pricing.

### 5. Preparation Time vs Delivery Time
The analysis revealed a gap between restaurant preparation time and actual delivery time.

This suggests that delivery performance should not be evaluated based on kitchen preparation alone. The transportation and delivery stages also play an important role.

## Business Insights and Recommendations

Based on the findings, the following areas deserve attention:

- Monitor delivery performance across different traffic conditions.
- Evaluate customer ordering patterns under different weather conditions.
- Review pricing and service fee structures across customer segments.
- Monitor restaurant preparation time and delivery time separately.
- Investigate operational bottlenecks between order preparation and customer delivery.

## Conclusion

This project reinforced the importance of connecting different business indicators to understand the bigger picture.

Order volume, restaurant performance, customer charges, and delivery time each provide useful information. However, analyzing them together creates a more meaningful understanding of the customer experience and operational performance.

**Key Takeaway:** Numbers tell you what happened. Good analysis helps you understand the story behind it.
